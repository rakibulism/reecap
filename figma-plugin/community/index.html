Reecap Motion FIGMA PLUGIN

Animate your Figma frames

Reecap Motion copies any frame into the free Reecap Motion Design tool — its text, shapes and groups arrive as editable layers, ready to animate and export as a real MP4.

◆ Install in Figma Open Reecap Motion →

How to use it

1
Select a frame in Figma

Any frame, component or group. The plugin shows a live 2× preview that updates as you change your selection.

2
Click “Copy to Reecap”

Your selection is rebuilt as an editable layer tree and copied to your clipboard. (Or use “Download PNG” to drag the image in.)

3
Open Reecap → Motion

Go to reecap.vercel.app/app/motion and paste with ⌘V / Ctrl+V — or Add Layer → Figma.

4
Animate & export

Give each layer an in/out animation preset with easing, scrub the timeline, and export a clean MP4.

What comes across

Text

Editable — content, size, weight, color and alignment are preserved.

Shapes

Rectangles & ellipses arrive as editable shapes with fill and corner radius.

Groups

Frames, groups, components and sections stay grouped and nested.

Everything else

Vectors, icons, images, gradients and effects are rasterized so they still look right.

🔒 Private by design. No backend, no network requests, no sign-in, and nothing stored — your selection is copied to your clipboard, that’s it.
